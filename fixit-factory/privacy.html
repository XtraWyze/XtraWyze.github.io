Fix-It Factory is a puzzle game. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, coins, materials, gems, boosters, workshop upgrades, daily reward history and settings are saved only on your device. We do not receive this information. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset local progress or by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the game.

If you turn on Local diagnostics in Settings, the game keeps a short event log on your device to help with troubleshooting. It is not sent to us. Turning the option off deletes the log.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, short ad breaks between some orders, and optional rewarded ads that you can choose to watch for in-game items. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice later with Settings → Ad privacy options when it is shown. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings.

Purchases

This edition of the game does not offer in-app purchases.

Analytics

This edition does not send analytics data to us.

Children

Fix-It Factory is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the game are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising described above, the game does not share information with third parties.

Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Information collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
